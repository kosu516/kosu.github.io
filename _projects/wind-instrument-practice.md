**Information and Communication Technology · Wearable Prototyping**

For our Cornerstone EECS Design and Implementation final project, we built a wearable that moves a wind instrument’s key layout onto a pair of gloves. A head-mounted airflow sensor measures breath input, so players can practice fingerings and breath control without the instrument.

Free Play lets players try fingerings on their own. Fingering Practice uses glove LEDs to guide players through finger positions. Song Performance cues notes in time with a song, much like a rhythm game. The project received an Excellence Award in the end-of-semester competition.
