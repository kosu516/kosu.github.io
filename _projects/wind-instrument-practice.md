## Introduction

For our Cornerstone EECS Design and Implementation final project, we built a wearable that lets musicians practice wind-instrument fingering and breath control on a tabletop. The device received an Excellence Award in the course competition.

{% include figure.liquid path=page.img class="project-hero" loading="eager" alt="The practice device's wired gloves and head-mounted breath sensor" %}

## Wearable controls

Buttons attached to the fingers reproduce the instrument’s key layout. The player presses them against a flat surface, while a head-mounted airflow sensor measures breath input to simulate volume. LEDs on the gloves cue which fingers to press.

## Practice modes

- **Free Play** lets players try fingerings at their own pace.
- **Fingering Practice** uses the LEDs to guide players through finger positions step by step.
- **Song Performance** cues the fingerings at a song’s tempo so players can follow along.
