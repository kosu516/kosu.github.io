**Robotics · Computer Vision · Hardware Prototyping**

Our team built a robotic arm that imitates the playful movements of a Turkish ice cream vendor. When a person reaches for the cone, hand tracking feeds their position into the motor control system so the arm can move the cone out of reach. I worked on the mechanism, hand-tracking algorithms, and integration of vision and motor control.

The project earned the Best Maker Award at the May 2026 MakeNTU Makerathon, which drew 44 teams. The award included NT$50,000 in prize money.
